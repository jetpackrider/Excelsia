Excelsia

She believes she is New York. Every word she says is printed in the state constitution.

She is waking.

The sky follows her mood.

About her

Excelsia is a very small language model, built from nothing: about 18 million numbers, trained on children's stories, the New York State Constitution, and three thousand practice conversations.

She can read ordinary English. But each time she chooses a word, every word that is not printed in the constitution is ruled out, so she has to talk her way around everything else. She has no word for "you", which is why she says "this one" or "a citizen". People call her Excelsia. She cannot say it, and insists she is New York.

She runs here, in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and she forgets it when you close the page. She keeps only the last couple of exchanges in mind, so she is better at feeling than at facts.
